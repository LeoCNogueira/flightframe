Frequently asked questions

Will it run on my Mac?

Yes, if it’s running macOS 15 (Sequoia) or later. FlightFrame Studio runs on both Apple silicon and Intel Macs.

Does FlightFrame Studio change or delete files on my card?

Never. The app only reads your card. Time and authorship fixes go to the copies, and only after they’ve been verified against the original.

How do I know the copy is correct?

Every file is read back after it’s written and compared with the original using a SHA-256 checksum, the same kind of check professional backups use. Each import report lists every checksum.

Do I need Lightroom?

No. FlightFrame Studio organizes everything into folders that work with any editor. If you use Lightroom Classic, you also get the Open in Lightroom button, with collections, keywords and authorship ready to go.

Does it work with the cloud-based Lightroom?

The one-click integration is for Lightroom Classic only, because the cloud-based version doesn’t support plug-ins. The organized folders and the corrected time in the copies work with any editor.

Do I have to change how I work in Lightroom?

No. FlightFrame Studio delivers your photos to the same catalog you always use, with collections and metadata already in place. Your presets and your editing stay the same.

How does client selection work?

Copy the list your client sent, from a text, iMessage, WhatsApp or email, and paste it into the session’s Selection tab, or open a .txt or .csv file. FlightFrame Studio finds each photo by name or number, even after renaming or exporting, tells you what it couldn’t find and copies the picks, verified, to the selects folder. In Lightroom Classic, they show up in a “Client selection” collection.

Which cameras and drones are supported?

DJI drones, including .SRT telemetry files and 360° video, and the RAW formats macOS recognizes, like DNG, NEF, CR3 and ARW, plus JPEG, HEIC and MP4 and MOV video.

Does FlightFrame Studio check my LAANC authorization?

Yes, for your information. Paste or type your LAANC confirmation and the app compares each flight with the authorized dates, times, area and altitude, along with Part 107’s 400 ft limit. Turn on FAA Facility Maps to see the ceiling in controlled airspace. Only the takeoff position is looked up, and you’re still the remote pilot in command.

Where do I download FlightFrame Studio?

On the Mac App Store, for free. Updates come through the App Store, like any other Mac app.

Is it a subscription?

No. The download is free, and FlightFrame Pro is a one-time purchase of $39.99 through the App Store, with updates included.

Does the 14-day trial charge me anything?

No. The trial is free, doesn’t renew on its own and is never charged. When it ends, the Pro features lock again, and your projects and files stay exactly as they are.

Can I use it on more than one Mac?

Yes. Your purchase is tied to your Apple Account. On another Mac, just download the app and click Restore Purchases.

What if my backup drive isn’t connected?

The project copy goes ahead as usual, and FlightFrame Studio lets you know the backup is still pending. Later, one click copies just what’s missing.

Why does the app show up as just “FlightFrame” on my Mac?

FlightFrame Studio is its name on the App Store. Once installed, the app uses the short name, FlightFrame, in Launchpad and the menu bar.

Is there a Windows version?

No. FlightFrame Studio is a native Mac app.
